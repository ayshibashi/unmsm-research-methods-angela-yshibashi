# Research Paradigm Justification

**Session 1**
## Overview
Given that the Banco Central de Reserva del Peru is about to launch Phase 4 of its Retail Payments Interoperability Strategy, inspired by India's UPI platform, which introduces a centralized payment initiation model, our interest has grown because strengthening and modernizing the country's National Payments System has also increased the need to control and  mitigate regulatory risk.

Design a machine learning framework to enables the rapid classification of Peruvian banking regulatory ecosystem regarding Payment System, including regulations from SBS Superintendencia de Banca y Seguros, BCRP Banco Central de Reserva del Peru, ANPDP Autoridad Nacional de Proteccion de Datos Personales and PCM Presidencia de Concejo de Ministros. This regulations encompass digital transformation, AI governance requirements, cyberdefense regulations, and the emerging regulatory landscape affecting banking institutions.

The key innovation lies in the fact that, instead of reading and reviewing all the regulations to determine their relevance, the model simply classifies them.

Paradigm Selected

Design Science

Develop a continuous regulatory standards radar to detect, classify, and prioritize the regulatory obligations that banks must fulfill within the framework of the payment system. These include digital transformation, AI governance requirements, cyberdefense regulations, and the emerging regulatory landscape affecting banking institutions.

## Justification

The question is: Can we build a system that ranks the relevance of X to avoid reviewing all regulations and determining their relevance? The model simply clasifies them.

The Peruvian banking sector faces numerous regulatory restrictions; our strategy focuses on just four key institutions to begin this study.

- Superintendencia de Banca, Seguros y AFP (SBS)
- Banco Central de Reserva del Perú (BCRP)
- Autoridad Nacional de Protección de Datos Personales (ANPDP)
- Presidencia de Consejo de Ministros - Secretaría de Gobierno y Transformación Digital (PCM)

Currently, regulatory monitoring is largely manual and decentralized, encompassing risk, legal, compliance, security, technology, and business units.

This creates:
- Regulatory compliance risk
- AI governance gaps
- Delayed regulatory response
- Inconsistent interpretation
- Cybersecurity compliance deficiencies

## Key Assumptions


- The objective is to design a tool that allows for cross-referencing with all regulatory authorities to identify regulatory gaps and upcoming compliance deadlines for banks, and to propose the seven fundamental guidelines of the Hevner Guidelines.

- Relevance of the problem: The objective of this research is to develop technological solutions to address a relevant, unresolved, and important business problem that is constantly evolving and has been the subject of criticism in articles worldwide.

- Evaluation of the research: To demonstrate the usefulness, quality, and effectiveness of the model (or design artifact) for the regulatory compliance framework.
